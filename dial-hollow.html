Ranah

A rotary line with a small keeper asleep in the hub. Dial a name and it wakes to listen — then goes back to sleep once you hang up.

1 Pull any number clockwise toward the brass stop and let go. The dial spins back and clicks off the digit. 2 Tap the handset to open the line. The keeper climbs up to listen; tap again to send it back down. 3 Switch the sky along the top to see the hub react to the caller's weather.

Contacts, Recents, Keeper, and Sounds open as their own screens in the next pass of this prototype.

Prototype control — not part of the in-app UI. Jumps Dial Hollow into a ringing state so you can see how the keeper reacts.
